### Format and Page Limits

Based on ICLR 2026 guidelines (ICLR 2027 author guide not yet published as of 2026-07-22):

- **Submission:** 9 pages of main content
- **Camera-ready:** 10 pages
- References: unlimited, do not count toward page limit
- Appendix: unlimited, reviewers not required to read
- Double-blind review via OpenReview
- Ethics statement: up to 1 page, does not count toward limit
- Template: ICLR LaTeX template (check iclr.cc when CFP publishes, as ICLR bumped the limit to 10 pages for ICLR 2026 submission — confirm whether this carries forward)

## 3. arXiv Preprint Strategy

arXiv posting is standard practice in this community and separable from venue submission. Key timing considerations:

| Scenario | Recommended arXiv Timing |
|---|---|
| Submitting to ICLR 2027 (double-blind) | Post on or after the submission date (Sep 24, 2026). ICLR policy permits concurrent arXiv posting but requires the submission itself be anonymized. Do not post before abstract registration to avoid deanonymization concerns. |
| Submitting to NeurIPS 2026 workshop | Post concurrent with or shortly after workshop acceptance notification (Sep 29, 2026). Workshop papers are typically not anonymized. |
| Submitting to SaTML 2027 (double-blind) | SaTML enforces double-blind; follow the same discipline as ICLR: post no earlier than the submission deadline. |
| TMLR submissions | TMLR explicitly permits concurrent arXiv posting at any point in the process. |

**Per-paper arXiv recommendation:**

- **Paper 1 (Circuit Tracing):** This builds directly on the Wang et al. (2022) IOI circuit work, which is a well-established arXiv baseline. Post to arXiv promptly to establish priority on the cross-model generalization finding. Target: September 24, 2026 (concurrent with ICLR 2027 submission).

- **Paper 2 (SAE Comparison):** The SAE space is moving quickly (TopK, Gated SAEs, JumpReLU all emerged in 2024–2025). Post early — the evaluation-confound finding has priority value. Target: concurrent with first venue submission, no later than October 2026.

- **Paper 3 (Distributed Interp):** The safety classifier application gives this paper visibility in the safety community beyond just ML conferences. Consider cross-posting to the Alignment Forum alongside arXiv. Target: concurrent with ICLR 2027 or SaTML 2027 submission.

### TMLR (Transactions on Machine Learning Research)

Rolling submissions via OpenReview. No conference cycle, no hard deadline — reviewed on a per-paper basis.

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Submission window | Year-round (holiday pause: approximately Dec 2 – Jan 5 each year) |
| Submission quotas | Enforced from July 1, 2026 — check current policy at jmlr.org/tmlr before submitting |
| Review timeline | Typically 4–6 months from submission to decision |
| Page limit | No strict page limit; papers should be as long as the content requires |
| Format | NeurIPS-style LaTeX template |
| Relationship to conferences | Accepted TMLR papers can be presented at ICLR (via "TMLR track"); strong community overlap |

**Strategic use:** TMLR is ideal as a primary venue for any paper that needs more than 10 pages to properly develop (e.g., the full SAE comparison with ablations across all evaluation metrics), or as a fallback if ICLR 2027 is rejected. It is not a fallback of lower prestige — TMLR acceptance carries weight in the interpretability community.

### Alignment Forum / LessWrong

Not peer-reviewed, but highly influential for Paper 3 (Distributed Interpretability). The safety classifier robustness finding is exactly the type of empirical result the alignment community engages with before journal publication. Post a writeup concurrent with arXiv for maximum feedback on framing.
